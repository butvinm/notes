# Idea

An idea records a possibility that is not decided and not scheduled: an improvement, an experiment, a question worth exploring. It exists so the thought is not lost and can be picked up when there is time.

## What to capture

- **Idea:** the proposal in one or two sentences, concrete enough to recognize later: what would change and where.
- **Why it might matter:** the problem it would solve or the opportunity it would open; the evidence in the packet that the problem is real.
- **Open questions:** what must be answered before the idea can become a decision: unknowns, risks, people to ask, things to measure.

## What is critical

The idea itself. Everything else is optional; an idea with an empty questions section is still worth keeping.

## How to reason

- Keep the tone tentative: an idea is not a decision, and the note must not read as if something was agreed.
- Put the names of the systems and techniques involved in `keywords`, so the idea surfaces when the developer works nearby.
- Tag with the project when the packet gives one; do not tag with a tracker issue unless the idea was raised in it.
- When the idea challenges an existing decision, name that decision in the body.
